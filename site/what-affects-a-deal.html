What affects a deal

Buyers weigh several things before they consider company data. These are the considerations we review on a call. Mode publishes $100K–$5M as its range; where in it you land depends on the data, the rights and the terms.

Rights to license

Can your company license this data? Contracts, customer terms and third-party content all matter.

Consent and personal data

Removing names does not make data anonymous. Personal information needs its own review.

Recency and history

Records that show how work changed over several years are usually more useful than a snapshot.

Volume and variety

Buyers look at how much data there is and how many kinds of work it covers.

Structure and context

Decisions, outcomes and revisions are easier to use than raw files with no context.

Terms and exclusivity

Scope, length of licence and payment timing shape the value of an offer, as well as the headline number.

Before any buyer sees your data

We check rights and personal information first. If something is unclear or cannot be licensed, we tell you before anything moves. You approve every buyer and every term.
