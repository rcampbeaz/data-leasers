What you can sell

AI teams look for real business records: how work was done, how decisions were made, and how problems were solved. Most of it sits in tools your team already uses.

Conversations

Threads where the question, the reply and the decision sit together.

  • Chat and call threads
  • Shared inboxes
  • Call transcripts
  • Decision threads

Customer support

Tickets from first message to the final fix.

  • Resolved tickets
  • Agent notes
  • Macros and replies
  • Escalations

Documents

Playbooks and reports, especially where earlier versions are kept.

  • SOPs and playbooks
  • Wikis
  • Proposals and reports
  • Revision history

Code and engineering

How code was changed, reviewed and tested.

  • Pull requests and reviews
  • Issues and fixes
  • Test history

Sales and CRM

Deal history and the reasons behind wins and losses.

  • Deal history
  • Call notes

  • Account activity

Operations

How work moved between people and teams.

  • Project boards

  • Exception logs

Typical buyer criteria

Buyers set their own requirements, and each deal is confirmed separately. As a starting point, many look for:

  • 30+ employeesCompanies with a sizeable working team.
  • 5+ years of historyEnough records to show how work changed over time.
  • Meaningful volumeOften a terabyte or more of documents, messages and records.
  • Any statusActive, restructuring or closed companies can all be considered.
