Who buys

Buyers are AI teams that need real business records to train and test their models. Mode reaches them and leads the discussion. You approve every buyer.

Model developers

Teams building general-purpose or domain-specific models that need realistic working data.

Applied AI teams

Companies building tools for a specific workflow, such as support, sales or operations.

Evaluation and research groups

Teams testing how well systems handle real problems, and needing benchmarks built from actual work.

Why buyers are not contacted directly

Introductions and terms go through Mode. This keeps negotiations, confidentiality and approvals in one place, and it means you only deal with buyers you have agreed to.
