Consent and contact preferences

What you agree to when you book a call, and how to change it.

Draft. Legal and privacy wording needs counsel review before launch.

What you agree to

When you submit the "Book a call" form, you agree that Data Leasers, LLC may:

  • store the details you enter so we can respond to your enquiry;
  • email you about arranging and following up on that call.

You will not receive marketing emails from the form unless you tick a separate box to opt in. Nothing you send us is shared with a data buyer or an AI company without your written approval.

Your choices

  • Withdraw consent at any time by emailing info@dataleasers.com.
  • Stop contact by replying "stop" to any email from us, or by asking us directly.
  • Delete your details by emailing us. We will confirm once they have been removed.

Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it. It may mean we cannot continue an enquiry.

Your data, your decision

Before any dataset is shared or licensed, we agree the terms with you in writing. Consent for that is separate from this form and is always requested on its own.

See also our privacy policy.
