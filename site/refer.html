What Mode pays

If the company becomes a qualified Mode data partner and the deal closes, you receive $50,000 or 6% of the partnership value, whichever is bigger.

These are Mode's stated terms. Your referral is confirmed in writing before any deal moves forward.

Who Mode is looking for

  • 30+ full-time employeesA company with a working team in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom or Australia.
  • 3+ years of operating historyEnough records to show how the business has run.
  • Multiple systems where business activity is storedEmail, documents, CRM, support tools or project boards.

Accounting, finance and medical practices can often qualify from 10+ employees, and law firms from 6+. Mode lists those thresholds separately.

How referrals work

  1. You make the introduction

    Tell us about a company that may be sitting on years of useful operating history. You do not need to know what its data is worth.

  2. We talk with them

    We hold the first call and check whether there is a fit.

  3. Terms in writing

    Your payment is confirmed in writing before any deal moves forward. We take it from there.
