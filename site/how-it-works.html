How it works

Three steps from a first conversation to an agreement you approve.

  1. We learn about your data

    A short call to understand what your company holds and what it could be worth.

  2. We introduce you to Mode

    If there is a fit, we make the introduction and hand over the conversation.

  3. Mode takes it forward, you decide

    Mode leads the discussion with buyers. Nothing is agreed without your sign-off.

Before anything moves

We check that the data can be shared and licensed before any buyer sees it. If that is not clear, we tell you.
