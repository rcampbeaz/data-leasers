Where to sell

Data does not go to the open market. It moves through one introduction at a time, with your approval at each step.

The routes a deal can take

Direct licence

One buyer licenses a defined dataset for a set period and purpose.

Structured package

Several records, cleaned and documented, offered together for a defined use.

Referral to a buyer

You introduce a company you know and it moves through the same process.

What does not happen

  • No public listingsYour data is not posted for anyone to bid on.
  • No handover before approvalNothing moves until you have agreed the buyer and the terms in writing.
  • No price on this pageValue depends on the data, the rights and the terms. We discuss it on a call.
