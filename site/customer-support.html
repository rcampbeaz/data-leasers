Customer support

Tickets from the first message to the final fix. AI teams use this kind of record to learn how real problems get resolved.

What it can include

  • Resolved tickets
  • Agent notes
  • Macros and replies
  • Escalations

What we check first

  • Customer detailsNames, contact details and account information need personal-data review.
  • Support tool termsSome help-desk platforms restrict reuse of ticket content. We check this first.
  • CompletenessTickets with a clear resolution are easier to use than open or half-finished ones.
