05  The process

From a first call to an agreement you approve.

How it works in detail
01

We learn about your data

A short call on what your company holds and whether it fits what the data buyer looks for.

02

We introduce you to the data buyer

If there is a fit, we make the introduction and hand over the conversation.

03

The data buyer takes it forward, you decide

The data buyer leads the discussion with AI teams. Nothing is agreed without your sign-off.
